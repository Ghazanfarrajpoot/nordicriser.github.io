Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

What counts as a message?

Each chat message you send in a room, or in 1:1 messaging, counts toward your monthly allowance. Translating it for other participants doesn't cost extra messages.

What happens when my free messages run out?

Your rooms, whiteboards, and message history stay fully accessible. Sending additional translated chat messages requires upgrading to a paid plan.

Which languages are supported?

Full interface support for English, Swedish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Dutch, Portuguese, Urdu, and Arabic, with chat translation available to further languages.

Is my data private?

Yes — messages and documents are handled under our GDPR-compliant Privacy Policy, which covers exactly what we collect and why.
