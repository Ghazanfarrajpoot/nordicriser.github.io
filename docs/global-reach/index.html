Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

How is usage measured?

Your plan includes a monthly session-minutes allowance. Time in a room counts from when you join a session until you leave it.

What happens when I use up my monthly minutes?

Your rooms and shared notes stay fully accessible. Starting a new timed session requires topping up or waiting for your next billing cycle.

Which languages are supported?

Full interface support for English, Swedish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Dutch, Portuguese, Urdu, and Arabic.

Is my data private?

Yes — your session data is handled under our GDPR-compliant Privacy Policy, which covers exactly what we collect and why.
