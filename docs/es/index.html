Process

From Brief to Launch — and Beyond

1

Discovery

We map what you actually need before writing any code. Scope, users, constraints, success criteria — documented and agreed.

2

Design & Architecture

Interface design and technical architecture in parallel. You see and approve the shape of the thing before we build it.

3

Build

Iterative development with working previews at every stage. No black-box six-week silences.

4

Launch

Deployment, domain and SSL setup, analytics, and handover documentation. You own everything.

5

Support

Ongoing maintenance, monitoring, and updates. Platforms don't stay healthy on their own.
