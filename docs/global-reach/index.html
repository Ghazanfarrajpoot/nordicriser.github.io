How It Works

Three Steps to Get Connected

Global Reach turns a subscription into direct, live access — no scheduling back-and-forth required.

1

Join a Room

Create or join a live room built around your topic — a consulting follow-up, a study session, or an open discussion.

2

Connect with Advisors & Peers

Collaborate in real time with advisors, specialists, or peers working through similar situations.

3

Get Structured Follow-Up

Leave each session with a clear record of what was discussed and what happens next.
