Sub-processors

Every third party we use to process personal data on your behalf — what they do, what data they touch, where they're located, and how the transfer is safeguarded.

Last Updated: 19 August 2026

This list exists to satisfy Article 13(1)(f) of the GDPR directly — not just to say safeguards exist, but to name who they're with. We keep it current and update the date below whenever it changes.

If we add a new sub-processor, we will update this list and, for clients with a Data Processing Agreement in place, give notice before the change takes effect, consistent with the objection right described in that agreement.
