# Nordic Riser logo

The mark is a front-facing lioness in an engraved ring: calm, alert, protective. She is drawn on a 100-unit grid. One line weight (3 units) is used for every ring and engraved cut, and her eyes, nose and mouth are cut out of a single flat shape. The wordmark is Space Grotesk Medium, tracked +10%. The STOCKHOLM line is IBM Plex Mono Medium. All lettering is outlined, so the files don't depend on any installed font.

Contrast (WCAG):

| Pairing | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Gold on navy | 6.22:1 |
| Navy on white | 14.22:1 |
| Navy on paper `#F9F7F2` | 13.28:1 |
| Deep gold on white | 5.65:1 |
| Ivory on navy | 12.07:1 |
| Gold on white | 2.29:1 (fails, so never put gold text on white; use navy or deep gold) |

Gold on navy is the primary pairing.

## Clear space

Keep clear space on every side equal to **x**, where x is one quarter of the medallion's diameter. On a 44 px-tall lockup that's 11 px. Nothing (text, edges, other logos) enters that zone.

## Don'ts

- Don't recolour outside the palette, add gradients, bevels, shadows or outlines, or set gold text on white.
- Don't stretch, skew, rotate or redraw the lioness, and don't add a mane, crown or extra detail.
- Don't rearrange the lockup, change the gap, or retype the wordmark in another font.
- Don't use the seal below 64 px; the lettering closes up. Use the medallion instead.
- Don't place the reverse lockup on light backgrounds or the primary lockup on navy.
- Don't put the logo on busy photos without a navy panel behind it.

## Trademark notes

The design deliberately avoids the marks it takes its feeling from. There's no leaping or side-profile pouncing cat, no crown or coronet, no overlapping double-R monogram and no winged figure. Keep it that way in any future edit.
