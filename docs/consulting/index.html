Consulting

Strategic Consulting for International Business Establishment

We provide structured, professional advisory across five core service areas — each designed to support entrepreneurs and professionals building a compliant business presence in Europe.

Home › Consulting
Our Client Profile

We Work With Four Distinct Client Groups

Our services are designed for professionals who require structured, compliance-grounded consulting — and who understand that building a business presence in Europe is a process that requires planning, documentation, and professional coordination.

01
Entrepreneurs

Entrepreneurs Planning to Relocate to Europe

This group consists of business founders and owner-operators who have decided — or are seriously considering — relocating to Europe through a structured business pathway. They typically have an existing business model or professional activity, and they understand that a credible, institutionally sound relocation strategy must be built on a properly established business foundation.

The challenge for most entrepreneurs in this group is not ambition — it is structure. Our role is to build that structure with them.

Common consulting needs in this group

Developing a business plan that meets institutional standards for relocation processes
Selecting the most appropriate legal entity and business structure for their situation
Understanding the regulatory and compliance framework relevant to their business activity
Coordinating legal, accounting, and documentation requirements in a structured sequence
Building a long-term operational strategy for their European business presence
02
Freelancers

Freelancers Working Internationally

Independent professionals — consultants, designers, developers, writers, and other knowledge-economy workers — increasingly operate across borders. Many in this group generate consistent professional income and work with international clients, but operate without the legal business structure that a European presence requires.

We work with freelancers to formalise their professional activity into a structured business framework — one that accurately reflects how they work and meets the expectations of European regulatory frameworks.

Common consulting needs in this group

Translating freelance activity into a formally structured, documentable business model
Understanding which legal structure is appropriate for a sole trader or micro-business
Preparing professional documentation that demonstrates viable and consistent business activity
Planning a compliant and sustainable path to establishing a legal business base in Europe
Coordinating with relevant legal and accounting professionals for formal registration steps
03
Professionals

International Professionals

This group includes senior professionals, executives, and specialists who are planning to establish long-term business operations in Europe — either as a continuation of their existing professional career or as the foundation for a new phase of their working life.

What they require is a structured consulting framework that translates their professional background into a viable and compliant European business model.

Common consulting needs in this group

Structuring professional experience and credentials into a formally viable European business model
Planning a corporate structure suited to long-term professional operations in Europe
Navigating cross-border compliance considerations relevant to their professional activity
Coordinating the documentation and planning required for a structured professional relocation
Establishing a business presence that positions them credibly within their professional sector
04
SME Owners

Small Business Owners Expanding to European Markets

Small and medium-sized business owners seeking to expand their operations into European markets face a set of strategic and structural challenges distinct from those of a startup or sole trader.

We help small business owners think through their European expansion strategically — assessing the right entry approach, structuring the legal and operational framework, and coordinating the professional support required.

Common consulting needs in this group

Developing a structured European market entry strategy suited to their business model
Selecting and designing the appropriate legal entity for European operations
Integrating a new European structure with their existing business operations and ownership
Managing cross-border compliance and regulatory requirements across jurisdictions
Coordinating legal, tax, and accounting professionals across the formation and setup process
What We Offer

Five Core Service Areas

Every service we provide is delivered through a structured consulting methodology — assessed individually, documented formally, and coordinated with qualified professional partners where required.

01
Relocation Strategy

Business Relocation Strategy Consulting

For entrepreneurs and professionals who intend to relocate to Europe through a structured business activity, this service provides strategic planning that begins with your business situation and works forward through the requirements of a legally compliant and institutionally defensible relocation process.

We assess your professional background, business model, and objectives, and develop a documented relocation strategy that integrates business structure, activity requirements, and long-term positioning. Our role is to ensure that every element of your plan is coherent, realistic, and aligned with regulatory frameworks — before any steps are taken.

We do not provide immigration advice or legal representation. We provide the strategic consulting framework from which qualified specialists can operate effectively.

This service covers

Relocation pathway assessmentBusiness activity planningRegulatory alignment reviewDocumentation strategyProfessional partner coordinationTimeline and milestone planning
02
Company Formation

Company Formation Strategy

Establishing a business entity in Europe requires careful strategic planning before any formal steps are taken. The choice of legal structure, jurisdictional considerations, ownership arrangements, and operational setup all have long-term implications — and errors at this stage are difficult and costly to correct.

This service provides structured consulting on how to approach company formation strategically. We help you understand the landscape of available structures, evaluate the most appropriate option for your situation, and develop a formation plan that supports both your immediate operational needs and your long-term business objectives.

Execution is coordinated with qualified legal and accounting professionals who handle the formal registration and compliance work within their licensed capacity.

This service covers

Legal entity evaluationStructure planning and selectionOwnership and governance designCompliance framework mappingBanking and financial setup guidanceLong-term positioning strategy
03
Business Documentation

Professional Business Plan Preparation

A business plan prepared for relocation or business establishment purposes is a formal document that must meet the expectations of European regulatory authorities, financial institutions, and professional reviewers. It is not a marketing document — it is a structured, evidence-based presentation of a viable business model.

We work with clients to develop business plans that accurately represent their activity, demonstrate operational viability, and are structured in line with institutional requirements. The process is methodical: we assess your business model, identify the documentation requirements relevant to your situation, and produce a plan that is coherent, credible, and professionally presented.

All business plans prepared through this service are developed to support formal processes — they are not generic templates, and they are not produced without a full understanding of the client's specific circumstances.

This service covers

Business model assessmentExecutive summary draftingMarket and activity analysisFinancial projections frameworkOperational structure documentationInstitutional formatting and review
04
Freelancer & Entrepreneur Advisory

Freelancer & Entrepreneur Relocation Consulting

Independent professionals, remote workers, and small business owners face a distinct set of challenges when planning to establish a legal business presence in Europe. Standard frameworks are often designed with larger entities in mind, and the pathway for a sole trader or micro-business requires a more tailored strategic approach.

This service is specifically designed for freelancers, remote professionals, and early-stage entrepreneurs who are building — or planning to build — a legal and sustainable business base in Europe. We help you understand what a compliant structure looks like for your professional profile, what documentation and planning is required, and how to position your activity credibly within regulatory frameworks.

We assess each client individually. What is appropriate for a software consultant differs significantly from what is appropriate for a creative professional or a product-based entrepreneur — and our advisory reflects that.

This service covers

Professional profile assessmentBusiness model formalisationCompliant structure designActivity and revenue documentationResidency-through-business planningLong-term sustainability review
05
International Advisory

Cross-Border Business Advisory

Entrepreneurs who operate across multiple countries face a more complex set of strategic and structural considerations. Questions of where to incorporate, how to manage cross-border transactions, how to structure ownership, and how to remain compliant across jurisdictions require careful and informed analysis — not generic answers.

This service provides advisory for internationally active business owners who need to think coherently about their multi-jurisdictional footprint. We help you map the relevant considerations, identify potential structural conflicts or opportunities, and develop a framework for managing your cross-border business activities in a way that is both operationally practical and compliant.

Where legal, tax, or regulatory specialist input is required — as it often is in cross-border matters — we coordinate engagement with qualified professionals in the relevant jurisdictions.

This service covers

Multi-jurisdiction structure reviewCross-border compliance mappingHolding and operating entity designInternational activity documentationTax consideration flaggingSpecialist partner coordination
